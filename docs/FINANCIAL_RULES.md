# Financial rules

Amounts require explicit unit, currency, period, accounting basis and actual versus guidance classification. Comparisons require matching definitions and periods. Operating cash flow minus cash capital expenditure is a defined free cash flow calculation; leased assets and financed purchases are separate. Companywide CapEx is not AI-only CapEx; cloud revenue is not AI-only revenue; RPO is not recognized revenue. Do not derive an exact AI ROI when AI-only input or output is undisclosed.
